# 🪝 Gravitational Hook (Fusion 360)

A hook and link mechanism designed in Autodesk Fusion 360, where gravity helps
drive the motion of the hook. The model is built as a proper assembly with
pivot joints, so it can be tested and animated.

## 📖 About
This project is a two-part mechanism made of a **hook** and a **link**,
connected through revolute (pivot) joints. The shape of the hook and the
position of its pivot let gravity swing it into place, so it can catch and
hold a load without needing a motor.

## ⚙️ Working Principle
- The **link** is the grounded part and acts as the support.
- The **hook** rotates about a pivot hole on the link.
- Gravity pulls the hook toward its resting position, and the curved
  profile catches and holds the load.

## ✨ Features
- Two parametric parts: `hook` and `link`
- Pivot holes designed for pin connections
- Revolute joint between the parts with limited range of motion
- Fully assembled model ready for motion study
